# Business Problem & Objectives

## The Scenario
ShopSphere, a mid-sized e-commerce company operating across India, has been generating substantial revenue. However, the management team lacked visibility into the actual profitability of their sales. They were operating blindly without understanding which regions, products, or customer segments were driving true business value.

## The Problem
* **Margin Erosion:** Management suspected that aggressive discounting was eroding profit margins, but could not prove which products were losing money.
* **Customer Blindness:** The marketing team did not know which customer segments (Consumer vs. Corporate) or age demographics to target for maximum ROI.
* **Lack of Timely Reporting:** Compiling sales reports manually in Excel took weeks, leading to delayed decision-making.

## The Objective
The goal of this Power BI project was to build an automated, interactive dashboard to:
1. Clean and standardize historical sales data.
2. Calculate accurate profit margins accounting for discounts and base costs.
3. Provide interactive drill-down capabilities for Category Managers.
4. Expose high-sales, low-profit products to optimize inventory and pricing strategy.
